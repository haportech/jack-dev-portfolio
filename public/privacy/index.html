Contact messages

The contact form sends your name, email address, and project message through EmailJS so I can receive and reply to your enquiry. EmailJS processes those details to deliver the message.

Include only what you want to share about your project. You can also contact me directly at hp@haportech.space.

Hosting and delivery

The hosting service may keep request logs, such as IP addresses, requested pages, and request times, to support delivery and security.

Website analytics

Cloudflare Web Analytics measures page visits and loading performance so I can understand how the site is used. Its analytics beacon does not use cookies or local storage to track individual visitors. Cloudflare explains its analytics practices.

What this site offers

This portfolio has no accounts, payments, or advertising. Project links take you to separate websites, which handle information under their own practices.
